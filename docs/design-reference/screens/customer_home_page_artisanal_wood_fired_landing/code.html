“True Neapolitan pizza cannot be rushed by industrial yeast or electronic heaters. It demands 48 hours of patience, cold wild fermentation, and the living fury of a 900°F beechwood flame.”

Born in the shadow of Mount Vesuvius, Matteo brought his family’s century-old biga mother starter to Forno & Farina. Every morning at 5:00 AM, our bakers hand-mill organic heritage wheat grains and hand-stretch every dough ball. We do not compromise, because great dough is a sacred covenant with time.
